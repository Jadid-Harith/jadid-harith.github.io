A personal publishing tool for short-form video.

Ember Station helps one creator, Jadid Harith, prepare short video clips and publish them to his own channels on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What it does

  • Prepares short vertical clips from footage provided by brands and creators who run public clipping campaigns.
  • Lets the operator review every clip and approve it by hand before anything is published.
  • Publishes approved clips to the operator's own accounts using each platform's official API, with the operator's permission.
  • Records which clips were published, so the operator can track their performance.

Who it is for

Ember Station is a private tool. It only connects to accounts owned by its operator. It is not offered to the public, and no other user can sign up for it.
